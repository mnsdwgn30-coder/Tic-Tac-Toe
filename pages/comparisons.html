🔵 Tic-Tac-Toe vs Connect 4

Key Differences & Mechanics

While both games involve building an aligned sequence of pieces, Connect Four adds gravity. You can only play in the lowest available slot of each column, which creates vertical columns of support and tactical "zugzwang" (forcing the opponent to play beneath you).

Connect Four was proven by James D. Allen in 1988 to be a forced win for the first player if they start in the middle column.

Engine Implementation Hierarchy

  • 🔹 Medium AI: Blocks immediate 2-in-a-row threats, but fails to anticipate double-forks.
  • 🔹 Hard AI: Uses heuristic evaluations and center/corner priorities with a 15% noise rate.
