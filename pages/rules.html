📜 Standard & Variant Rules

How To Play Tic-Tac-Toe

From fundamental beginner rules to tournament regulations, reverse Misère modes, and common misconceptions debunked.

🎯 The Golden Objective

Tic-Tac-Toe is a two-player zero-sum game played on a 3×3 grid. One player marks X and the other marks O. The first player to align three of their marks horizontally, vertically, or diagonally wins the match. If all 9 squares are filled with no triple formed, the game ends in a Draw (Tie / Cat's Game).

🐣 Beginners

Basic Rules for Beginners

  • 🔹 Grid Setup: Draw a 3×3 grid with 9 blank cells.
  • 🔹 Turns: Players take turns placing exactly one symbol per turn in an empty cell.
  • 🔹 First Turn: Traditionally, player X moves first. In subsequent rounds, players alternate first move.
  • 🔹 Permanent Moves: Once placed, a mark cannot be moved, replaced, or erased.
🎈 Kids Edition

Rules for Kids (Simplified)

Teaching younger children? Use these intuitive guidelines:

  • 🟢 "Three in a Line": Think of it like connecting train cars in a row!
  • 🟢 "Block the Bridge": If you see two of your friend's symbols together, put yours in the empty spot to stop them from winning.
  • 🟢 No Peeking / No Double Moves: Wait for your turn after your friend takes theirs.
🏆 Competition

Official Standard Regulations

In competitive and tournament formats:

  • ⏱️ Time Control: Standard blitz turns allow 5 to 10 seconds per move.
  • ⚖️ Series Scoring: Matches are played in sets of 10 or 20 games; 1 point for a win, 0.5 points for a draw.
  • 🔁 Alternating Openings: Players must swap who plays 'X' after each individual game.
🌀 Variants

Variant Rules (Misère, 4x4, 3D)

  • 🔄 Misère (Reverse Mode): Getting 3-in-a-row causes you to lose! You must force your opponent to complete a line.
  • 📐 4x4 Grid: 16 cells. Requires 4-in-a-row to win. Higher complexity and fewer draws.
  • 🧊 3D Tic-Tac-Toe (Qubic): Played on a 4×4×4 cube (64 cells) with 76 winning lines.
🔍 Clarifications

Common Rule Mistakes & FAQs

Can a player skip a turn or pass? ▼

No. In standard Tic-Tac-Toe rules, passing is strictly forbidden. A player must place their symbol on an available square whenever it is their turn.

What happens if both players create 3-in-a-row at the same time? ▼

This is physically impossible in standard play because moves are made sequentially one at a time. The first player who places their third symbol to complete a line immediately wins the game, and play terminates on the spot.

Why is a Draw called "Cat's Game"? ▼

The term dates back to 19th-century English idioms referring to "cat's play" or a game where neither side can prevail, likening it to an agile cat that always lands on its feet or plays with its tail without a clear victor.
