📖 Terminology Index

Glossary of Terms

Definitions and tactical breakdowns for essential game concepts, mathematical terms, and algorithmic heuristics.

Tactics

Fork

A tactical move that creates two simultaneous winning threats (two unblocked 2-in-a-rows). Because the opponent can only block one square on their next turn, a fork guarantees victory.

Defense

Block

Placing a mark in an empty square to stop an opponent who already has two marks aligned in a row, column, or diagonal from winning on their upcoming turn.

Algorithms

Minimax

A recursive decision-making algorithm in artificial intelligence used in two-player zero-sum games to find the optimal move assuming the opponent also plays optimally.

Algorithms

Alpha-Beta Pruning

An optimization technique for the Minimax algorithm that eliminates branches in the search tree that cannot influence the final decision, dramatically increasing speed.

Theory

Optimal Play

A sequence of moves where both players make the mathematically best decision at every turn. In Tic-Tac-Toe, optimal play always terminates in a Draw.

Rules

Cat's Game (Draw / Tie)

A finished game where all 9 squares on the board have been filled without either player successfully achieving 3 of their marks in a row.

Geometry

Dihedral Group D4

The symmetry group of a square consisting of 8 operations (4 rotations and 4 reflections). In Tic-Tac-Toe, D4 reduces 5,478 board states to 765 unique classes.

Rules

Misère Mode

A game variant where standard victory conditions are inverted: the first player to form a 3-in-a-row loses the game.

Theory

Zero-Sum Game

A mathematical representation where one participant's gain or loss is exactly balanced by the losses or gains of the other participant.
